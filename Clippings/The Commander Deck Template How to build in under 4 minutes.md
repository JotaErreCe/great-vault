Commander deck template: how to build a Commander deck in under 4 minutes with a plug-and-play formula for any commander.  
  
Most Commander decks don't fail because of the cards. They fail because of the math. Here's the definitive template for ANY commander: 38 lands, 10 ramp, 10 card draw, 10 to 12 answers. That's about 70 cards of pure function, and the last 30 are yours: theme, synergy, finishers, pet cards. It's a starting point, not a law. Aggressive decks can trim lands, control decks can add draw.
